Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026  |  Tark Peer

This Privacy Policy describes how Tark Peer ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information about you when you use the Tark Peer mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who Can Use Tark Peer

Tark Peer is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18. If we learn that a user under 18 has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and password (stored as a hash by Supabase Auth).
  • Profile information: Age, gender, country, state/region, occupation, sector, mother tongue, and optional bio text.
  • Avatar: You select from a preset collection of DiceBear-generated avatar images. No custom photos are uploaded.
  • Call metadata: Call timestamps (start/end), call duration (in seconds), and ratings given after calls. Call history is stored to power your Progress tab statistics.
  • Chat messages: Text messages sent to friends within the app. All messages are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Presence data: Online/offline/in-call status, updated in real time and not stored beyond your session.
  • Moderation data: Reports you submit against other users, and any restriction or block actions you take.
  • Device token: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to deliver push notifications. Stored against your account and deleted when you delete your account.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Voice audio is not recorded or stored. Voice calls are transmitted in real time through Agora's peer-to-peer infrastructure and are never written to disk by us.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location. Country and state are entered manually by you.
  • We do not collect device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.).

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match you with conversation partners (profile fields are shared with matched partners).
  • To deliver real-time voice calls via Agora.
  • To enable friend connections, in-app chat, and direct calls.
  • To display progress statistics (total calls, streak, ratings).
  • To send push notifications about matches, friend requests, and peak-hour reminders.
  • To enforce our community standards through moderation (reports and restrictions).
  • To improve the app through aggregate, non-personal usage analytics.

5. Third-Party Service Processors

We share data with the following third parties strictly for operating the service:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Database, authentication, and real-time messaging. Your profile, call history, messages, and authentication credentials are stored on Supabase infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Agora (agora.io) — Real-time voice transmission. Agora processes voice audio in transit. Audio is not retained beyond the call duration. Agora Privacy Policy
  • Render.com — Cloud hosting for our backend API server. Render processes API requests on our behalf. Render Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Cloud Messaging) — Push notifications. FCM device tokens are sent to Firebase to deliver notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • DiceBear (dicebear.com) — Avatar image generation. Avatar URLs are fetched from DiceBear's public CDN. No personal data is sent. DiceBear Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC) — AI language model powering Shivani, the AI practice partner. During AI practice sessions, your spoken words are converted to text on your device by the speech-to-text engine; that text is then sent to Google's Gemini API to generate Shivani's replies. Voice audio itself is never transmitted to Google — only the text transcript of what you say. Conversation text is not used to identify you and is not linked to your account by Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — App distribution and in-app update mechanisms on Android devices.

6. Data Retention

  • Chat messages are automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Call history (metadata only) is retained until you delete your account.
  • Profile data is retained until you delete your account.
  • Reports you have submitted are retained for moderation purposes even after account deletion; reports made against you may be retained for safety reasons.
  • FCM tokens are deleted when you log out or delete your account.

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app: Profile → Delete Account.

Deletion removes your profile, display name, chat messages, friend relationships, call ratings, and block/report data you created. Call history rows involving you are anonymised (your name will show as "Unknown" in your former partners' history). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

You may also request deletion by email at support@tarkpeer.example. We will process emailed deletion requests within 30 days.

8. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Supabase uses row-level security (RLS) policies so each user can only access data they are authorised to see. We do not store passwords — authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to certain uses of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@tarkpeer.example.

10. Applicable Law

This policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Where applicable, we comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
